## Title
AI Reimagines: The Motown Revue That Never Landed — It Just Kept Playing in Orbit

## Hook
Cold open on a golden-lit stage inside a slowly rotating space station: a full
horn section holds formation in zero-g, brass catching light like a mirror
ball, artificial gravity spinning the room the way a record spins on a
turntable.

## Caption
What if the Motown revue never came back down to Earth — it just kept
touring, one station further out each decade, horns and harmonies drifting
weightless under stage lights that never dim? This is a fully original
AI-composed soul track built in that classic Motown horn-and-groove pocket,
scored against a visual world where the whole revue plays out in zero
gravity — no sampled record, no lifted vocal, just a new song built from
scratch in the shape of that sound, same rule we hold every time. Where do
you think this tour stops next: the Moon, or further out? 🌕🎷
