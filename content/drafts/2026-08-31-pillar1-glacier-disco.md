## Title
AI Reimagines: The Disco Night That Never Ended — It Just Moved Into a Melting Glacier

## Hook
Cold open on a mirrorball hanging inside a hollowed-out glacier cave, its
light scattering off blue ice walls instead of a ceiling, a full disco floor
built into the frozen floor as meltwater drips through the beams like rain
through a spotlight.

## Caption
What if a 1970s disco never got a last call — it just relocated, floor by
floor, into the last cold place left on Earth, glowing under a mirrorball
hung from solid ice? This is a fully original AI-composed disco track,
built from scratch in that classic four-on-the-floor strings-and-falsetto
pocket — no sampled record, no lifted vocal, same rule we hold every time —
scored against a visual world where the whole party is slowly melting the
room it's dancing in. Does the party outlast the glacier, or does the
glacier win? ❄️🪩

Follow @the_shedstudio for the next one.
