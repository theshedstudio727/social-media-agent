## Title
AI Reimagines: The Early-2000s Pop-Punk Band That Never Made It Off the Parking Lot Circuit

## Hook
Cold open on a strip-mall parking lot at dusk, sodium lights buzzing on, a
four-piece unloading a van covered in band stickers while a folding table
of burned CDs sits next to a hand-painted banner — then the first chord
hits and the whole lot fills in like it's a sold-out room.

## Caption
What if the band that never got past the all-ages-show-in-a-parking-lot
stage had actually been this good the whole time — you just never caught
one of the four shows they played before the guitarist moved for a real
job? This is a fully original AI-composed track, built from scratch in
that early-2000s pop-punk pocket — palm-muted verses, a chorus that's all
gang vocals and one drumstick click-in, a bridge that drops to just bass
and hi-hat before the last chorus doubles up — no sampled record, no
lifted vocal, same rule we hold every time — scored against a visual world
where the parking lot lighting rig is literally just the store signage and
someone's headlights. Would you have been in that lot in 2003, or are you
too old for the mosh pit now? 🛹🔥
