## Title
AI Reimagines: The New Wave Show That Never Got Rained Out — It Just Moved to the Top of a Lighthouse

## Hook
Cold open on a small synth-pop band silhouetted at the top of a storm-battered
lighthouse, gear lashed to the railing, the lighthouse's own rotating beam
sweeping the stage like a spotlight as waves smash the rocks below.

## Caption
What if an 80s New Wave show never got cancelled for weather — it just
climbed to the top of the last lighthouse standing and kept playing, running
purely on the beam that's supposed to be warning ships away? This is a fully
original AI-composed synth-pop track, built from scratch in that classic
gated-drum, chorus-guitar, analog-synth pocket — no sampled record, no
lifted vocal, same rule we hold every time — scored against a visual world
where the storm keeps trying to drown out the set and the light keeps
cutting back through. Does the storm ever actually win, or does the beam
just keep the show going all night? ⚡🎹

Follow @the_shedstudio for the next one.
