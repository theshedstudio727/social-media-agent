## Hook
"Five random words, sixty seconds, zero retakes — watch the clock, not the camera."

## Caption
Every freestyle clip this persona has dropped gets the same comment: "that's edited, nobody just does that." So this one strips away every excuse — five words get revealed on screen only when the sixty-second countdown starts, all five have to land somewhere in the verse before time runs out, and the take either works or it gets posted exactly as it fell apart. No producer cutting around a stumble, no retake if a word doesn't land clean. It's less about the bars being perfect and more about watching the character think in real time with a deadline bearing down. If this clip holds up, send the next five words yourself — DM @the_shedstudio your toughest word list and the next countdown freestyle runs on whatever you pick. Which word do you think breaks the run first?

## Production Notes
Single continuous take, vertical framing, on-screen countdown timer overlay (60→0) with the five random words appearing one at a time as they're used/called out, synced to a visible digital clock graphic. Use the established persona character reference for face/voice consistency with prior Pillar 3 clips. Underlay a generic, non-licensed boom-bap beat bed generated fresh for this clip (not a real song, no sampling) — copyright/likeness guardrail: reimagined generic beat style only, never a specific existing track or a real artist's voice/likeness. Needs synced dialogue over the beat plus the timer/word-reveal graphic overlay in post.
