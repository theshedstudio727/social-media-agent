## Title
AI Reimagines: The 90s Grunge Set That Got Rained Out — So It Moved Into the Botanical Greenhouse

## Hook
Cold open on a glass-domed Victorian greenhouse at night, rain hammering the
panes, a four-piece set up between the ferns with amps duct-taped against
the humidity and condensation running down every pane like the whole room
is sweating with the crowd.

## Caption
What if the outdoor grunge show that got rained out at the last minute
never actually got cancelled — it just relocated into the one building on
the festival grounds with a roof, the old botanical greenhouse, plants and
all? This is a fully original AI-composed grunge track, built from scratch
in that early-90s pocket — detuned power chords, a bassline that sits low
and dirty, drums mixed like they're in a room too small for them — no
sampled record, no lifted vocal, same rule we hold every time — scored
against a visual world where the glass roof turns into the only lighting
rig and the rain becomes part of the mix. Would you rather have caught the
original outdoor set or this rained-in version under glass? 🌿⚡

Follow @the_shedstudio for the next one.
